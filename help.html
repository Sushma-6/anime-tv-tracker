Help & FAQ

Getting Started

👉 Add anime or TV shows to your watchlist using the My List page.

👉 Track your episode progress directly from the anime detail page.

👉 Join clubs to chat with other fans and participate in polls.

Common Questions

  • How do I hide spoilers? → Use the toggle button on the spoilers page.
  • Can I vote in polls? → Yes! Go to the Polls page in your club.
  • How do I reset my progress? → Edit your watchlist entry anytime.
