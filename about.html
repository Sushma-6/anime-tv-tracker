About AnimeTracker

Our Mission

AnimeTracker is built to make it easier for fans to manage their anime & TV series watchlists, track progress, and connect with others through clubs, polls, and discussions.

Features

  • 📌 Watchlist Categories (All, Watching, Completed, Dropped)
  • 📊 Track episode progress
  • 💬 Join clubs and discussions
  • ⚡ Participate in polls & reviews
  • 🚀 Simple, fast, and responsive design
